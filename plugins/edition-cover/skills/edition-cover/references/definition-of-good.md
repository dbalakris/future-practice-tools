# What a good cover is

Changes faster than the skill; edit freely.

## The job

The cover is the image at the top of each edition and the card in the newsletter's list. Its composition never changes between editions, so a reader recognizes the newsletter before reading the title. Only the number, title and subtitle change.

## Must hold (the script checks these)

- Title on two lines at most, inside the sheet's margins.
- Subtitle on one line, two at most.
- Nothing runs off the sheet.

## Should hold (check by eye)

- The break keeps a phrase together. "Hours saved isn't / a business strategy", not "Hours saved / isn't a business strategy".
- The subtitle states the position in plain words. Not a question, not a teaser.
- The subtitle doesn't open with the title's own words. If it does, tell the user and suggest one alternative; don't change it unasked.
- Title in sentence case, exactly as the piece is titled.

## Decided, don't relitigate

- One weight for the whole title. Bold phrases were tried and dropped (Oct 2026); `title_bold` in the config can turn them back on.
- The mark sits small at the top right of the sheet, on the series line. A large mark straddling the sheet's edge read as misplaced.
- No photos, gradients or shadows. The second sheet behind gives the depth.
- 1920 x 1080 PNG. LinkedIn crops the preview in the feed, so the title, not the mark, has to carry the image.
